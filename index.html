GORE BOX

PHYSICS PLAYGROUND · 2D SANDBOX
↻ Turn your phone sideways for the best view
▶ arrow opens / closes the spawn menu. Drag an item out into the world (or tap it to drop one in).
Drag anything to move it. Flick and let go to throw. Use two fingers on a blade to rotate it; use two fingers on empty space to pan and zoom.
Guns: select one, then press the FIRE button. Rifle & minigun can toggle full-auto by tapping the gun.
Tap grenades / C4 to arm them. Shoot barrels to blow them up.
Swing blades fast into people to cut, stab or decapitate. Tap a stuck blade to rip it out.
📌 Pin tool: tap an item to freeze it in the air (drag its barrel end to aim a frozen gun), or tap a body part to nail it in place.
🔥 Fire tool: tap anywhere to start a fire. Flames spread, burn characters and can ignite explosive barrels. 🗑 Delete tool: tap to remove things. SLO = slow motion.
Zombies hunt humans; humans run from zombies.
iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen to play full screen like an app.
